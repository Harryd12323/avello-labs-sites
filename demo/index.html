What you're looking at

  • ⚑Red notes are things a person should double-check, like a stop over an hour, a break at a place that isn't a store, or a GPS that didn't record the truck turning off at the end of the day.
  •  A yellow mark at the right of a store means that visit ran longer than an hour.
  • 1ST1ST / 2ND split the day at the break, so TOTAL HOURS leaves the break out.
How it runs every day

Set it up once. Then it's 10 seconds a day.

1

Reveal emails the daily GPS report

Each morning, Verizon Reveal sends it as a scheduled report.

2

Someone saves it to the inbox folder

Save the email attachment into a folder on the office PC. It takes about 10 seconds. Later, an email rule can do this step for you.

3

The log fills itself out

The tool fills in the weekly log automatically and flags anything odd.
