What the numbers mean

Most of what your website earns comes down to two things. How many people find it, and how many of them get in touch. If either one slips, you lose customers you never hear about.

The calculator shows what one more customer a month is worth to you. That's the number to keep in mind when you decide what to spend on your site.

What usually costs you calls

  • A contact form that quietly stopped sending. It looks fine, but messages never arrive.
  • A site that's slow or awkward on a phone. People give up and call the next name.
  • Old hours, services or prices. Customers see them and go elsewhere.
  • Not showing up when people nearby search. They can't call you if they can't find you.
