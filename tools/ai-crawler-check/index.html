Search bots and training bots do different jobs

AI companies send out more than one kind of bot, and blocking them has very different effects:

  • AI search bots, like OAI-SearchBot, Claude-SearchBot and PerplexityBot, find pages to show in AI answers. If they're blocked, you're much less likely to be recommended. This is the block that hurts.
  • Training bots, like GPTBot, ClaudeBot and CCBot, collect pages that may be used to train AI models. Blocking them is a fair choice and doesn't keep you out of AI search.
  • Opt-out tokens, Google-Extended and Applebot-Extended, aren't bots at all. They tell Google and Apple whether your pages can be used for AI training, without touching search.
  • Classic search bots, Googlebot and Bingbot, also feed Google's AI Overviews and Microsoft Copilot. Blocking them takes you out of search entirely.

How to fix a block

Your robots.txt lives at yourbusiness.com/robots.txt. To let AI search bots in while opting out of training, the rules can look like this:

User-agent: OAI-SearchBot
User-agent: Claude-SearchBot
User-agent: PerplexityBot
Allow: /

User-agent: GPTBot
User-agent: ClaudeBot
User-agent: CCBot
Disallow: /

User-agent: *
Allow: /

Sitemap: https://yourbusiness.com/sitemap.xml

On WordPress, an SEO plugin usually has a robots.txt editor. On Wix and Squarespace, look in the SEO settings. Changes can take a day or more to be noticed.

What this check can't see. robots.txt is a request that well-behaved bots follow. Firewalls, hosting settings and options like Cloudflare's AI bot blocking can turn bots away before robots.txt matters, and this check can't see those. Bot names and purposes change too; each one links to the company's own documentation.

Common questions

What is robots.txt?

A small text file at yourbusiness.com/robots.txt that tells crawlers which parts of your site they may visit. Well-behaved bots follow it, but it's a request, not a lock.

Should I block AI crawlers?

It depends what they do. Blocking AI search bots like OAI-SearchBot or PerplexityBot can keep you out of AI answers, which most local businesses want to be in. Blocking training bots like GPTBot only opts you out of model training.

Does blocking GPTBot keep my site out of ChatGPT?

Not by itself. OpenAI uses OAI-SearchBot for ChatGPT search results. GPTBot collects pages for training.

What is Google-Extended?

A control token rather than a separate crawler. It tells Google whether your pages can be used to train Gemini and to ground its answers. It doesn't affect Google Search or your rankings.

My robots.txt allows everything. Why might AI tools still not see my site?

Firewalls and settings like Cloudflare's AI bot blocking can turn bots away before robots.txt comes into it. This check can only read robots.txt, so if AI tools still can't see you, check your hosting and security settings too.
