# Get found on Google and in AI answers
Subtitle: A checklist for local businesses

When someone nearby searches for what you do, or asks an AI tool for a recommendation, you want your business to be one of the names that comes up. This checklist covers the basics that help, in the order we'd tackle them.

## 1. Your Google Business Profile
Google says local results are based mainly on three things: relevance, distance and prominence. Your Business Profile feeds all three.
- Claim and verify your profile at business.google.com.
- Pick the most specific primary category that fits, then add any secondary ones that truly apply.
- Fill in your hours, service area, phone number and website, and keep holiday hours up to date.
- Add real photos of your work, your team and your location.
- Ask happy customers for reviews, and reply to every review, good or bad.

## 2. Make sure search engines and AI tools can read your site
- Search your business name plus your town. If your site doesn't show up at all, Google may not have it indexed. Google Search Console will tell you.
- Check your robots.txt file isn't blocking Google or the AI search bots. Our free AI crawler check at avellolabs.com/tools/ai-crawler-check/ shows which bots your site lets in.
- Make sure your site loads on https, with no security warning.

## 3. Say clearly what you do and where
- Every page needs a title that says what the page is about, plus your town.
- Your home page should say in the first line what you do and the area you serve.
- Give each main service its own page, written the way customers talk about it.
- Put your name, address and phone number on the site exactly as they appear on your Business Profile.

## 4. Answer the questions customers actually ask
AI tools build their answers from pages that answer a question directly.
- List the questions you hear on the phone every week, and answer each one on your site in a few plain sentences.
- Add prices or price ranges if you can. Customers ask about them first.
- Add business details in a format search engines read (called schema). Our free schema generator at avellolabs.com/tools/schema-generator/ writes it for you.

## 5. Build trust beyond your own site
- Keep your business name, address and phone number the same everywhere you're listed.
- Get listed in the directories and associations that matter in your trade.
- Local news, sponsorships and partner businesses that link to you all help Google and AI tools see you as a real, active business.

## What to skip
- Anyone who promises a top spot on Google. Nobody controls that.
- Pages stuffed with town names you don't really serve.
- Buying reviews or links. Google's rules forbid both.
