## What gets in the way
- **Word of mouth only goes so far.** Referrals are great, but new neighbors and new towns find you through Google, and now through AI answers too.
- **Your work is local, but your site doesn't say where.** If your site never names the towns you serve, Google has less to go on when someone nearby searches.
- **Quote requests are a hassle.** A form that's long, broken or buried means people give up and message someone else.
- **You're too busy working to update the site.** Old prices, last year's services and a dead Facebook link make you look closed.

## What we do about it
We build a simple, fast site that answers what customers want to know. What you do, where you work, and how to get a quote. Your phone number is one tap away on every page.

Each main service gets its own page, so a search for “panel upgrade” or “lawn care” lands on the right one. We add pages for the towns you want more work in, whether that's Cherry Hill, Moorestown or across the river in Philadelphia. Quote forms ask only what you need and come straight to you. We test them every month.

Your Google Business Profile matters as much as your site. Google says local results are based mainly on relevance, distance and prominence. We keep your services, areas and hours current, so your listing matches your site.

## Busy season, slow season
Landscapers live by the calendar. So do painters, cleaners and pest control. We plan the site around yours. Spring cleanups up top in March, leaf removal in the fall, gutter cleaning before winter.

On the Growth plan, each month brings one new page or post aimed at a search your customers make, plus a fresh post and photos on your Business Profile. It keeps your name in front of people when they're ready to book.

## Common questions
Q: I get most of my work from referrals. Do I need SEO?
A: Referrals are the best kind of work. But even a referred customer often looks you up before calling. A clear site and profile help that call happen.
Q: Should I add online booking?
A: If your jobs are simple to book, like a cleaning or an inspection, it can help. For quote-based work, a short quote form usually fits better.
Q: Can one site cover several services?
A: Yes. If you do a few related things, each one gets its own page, and the homepage ties them together.
Q: What does the monthly plan cover for a small crew?
A: Care keeps the site up, backed up and your forms working, with your changes made on Pro and Premium. Growth adds steady work on getting found nearby.
