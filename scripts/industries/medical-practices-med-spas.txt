## What gets in the way
- **Your treatments are hard to find.** One page lists everything, so a person searching for a specific treatment near Cherry Hill never lands on you.
- **The site looks dated next to your front desk.** Patients judge the care by the website before they ever walk in.
- **Booking takes too many steps.** If they can't see how to book from their phone, they try the next name on the list.
- **Your details don't match.** Hours, phone numbers or addresses differ between your site, Google and the directories, and Google gets less sure about you.
- **The contact form asks for too much.** A plain website form isn't the place for symptoms or medical history.

## What we do about it
We give each treatment or service its own clear page, written the way patients ask about it. That gives Google and AI tools something specific to show when someone nearby searches for it.

We make booking obvious on a phone. One tap to call, or a clear link to the booking system you already use.

We keep your name, address, phone and hours the same everywhere, starting with your Google Business Profile. Google says local results depend on relevance, distance and prominence, and clear, matching details help with the first and the last.

And we keep the site fast and up to date every month, so it never looks forgotten.

## Keeping health details off the website
Your website should help people get in touch, not collect their health history. We keep contact forms short: a name, a way to reach them and a time that suits. Anything clinical belongs in the patient systems you already use.

We're not your compliance adviser. If you're unsure what your site should or shouldn't collect, check with your own advisers, and we'll build it the way they say.
