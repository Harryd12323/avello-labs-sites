## What gets in the way
- **Trust comes first.** Homeowners are about to spend real money and let a crew on their house. A thin site with stock photos gives them a reason to keep looking.
- **Storms bring a rush of searches.** After a bad storm, everyone searches for roof repair at once. If your site is slow or your number is buried, those calls go elsewhere.
- **Big jobs take time to decide.** People look at photos, read about materials and compare companies before they ask for an estimate. Your site has to answer their questions.
- **Estimate requests leak.** A form that asks too much, or doesn't reach you, loses jobs you never hear about.

## What we do about it
We build a site that shows people who you are. Real photos of your crews and finished roofs. Your license and insurance details where people can see them. Your reviews linked from the site, so homeowners can see what others say about you.

You get a page for each kind of job, like roof replacement, repairs, gutters and siding, plus pages for the towns you work in most. A homeowner in Cherry Hill looking for a roof replacement should land on a page that speaks to them, not a generic list.

Your number sits at the top of every page, one tap to call. The estimate request form is short, comes straight to you, and we test it every month. When storm season hits, tell us and we can put a clear storm damage message on the site.

## Showing your work
Photos sell roofs. On the Growth plan, we add photos you send from finished jobs to your site and your Google Business Profile, with a line on what you did and where. Over time, that builds a portfolio of real work across South Jersey.

The one new page or post a month can answer what homeowners ask you on every estimate. How long a roof lasts. Repair or replace. What happens on install day. Pages like that help people trust you before you've met.

## Common questions
Q: Should I put prices on my roofing website?
A: You don't have to list exact prices. A page on what affects the cost of a roof, and how your estimates work, answers the question people are really asking.
Q: Do before and after photos help?
A: Yes. They show your real work. Use photos of your own jobs, with the homeowner's OK, and add them to your Google Business Profile too.
Q: Can you help after a storm when calls spike?
A: We can update your site quickly, for example a storm damage notice and a clear way to request an inspection. On Pro and Premium, those changes are part of your monthly edits.
