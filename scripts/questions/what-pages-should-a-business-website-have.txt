## The short answer
A small business site needs four kinds of page to start:

- **Home:** what you do and where, in the first few lines
- **A page for each main service:** what it is, who it's for and how to book
- **About:** who you are, so people know who they're dealing with
- **Contact:** phone, email, address or service area, and hours

Once those are in place, add a page for each area you serve and a page that answers the questions customers ask.

## Why a page for each service matters
Google says local results are based mainly on relevance, distance and prominence. A clear page for each service helps Google see how well you match a search. If you're a plumber in Cherry Hill, a page about water heater repair can show up for that search. A single page that lists ten services usually can't.

## Pages that can wait
A blog is nice but not needed on day one. Start with the pages above. On our Growth plan, we add one new page or post a month, aimed at something your South Jersey customers actually search for.

## Common questions
Q: How many pages does a small business site need?
A: It depends on how many services and areas you cover. Our websites start at 5 to 7 pages, which covers the basics for a lot of local businesses.
Q: Should I put prices on my website?
A: If you can share them, it helps. People and AI tools both look for what things cost.
Q: Do I need a page for every town I serve?
A: Only for the places you really work in, and only if each page says something useful about that area. Copy-and-paste town pages don't help anyone.
