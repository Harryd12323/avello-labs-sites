## The short answer
Getting on the map and getting found on the map are two different things. Your business appears on Google Maps once you've created a Google Business Profile and Google has verified it. Google's Business Profile help says new profiles and edits are reviewed, and it can take some time before they show.

Showing up when someone in Cherry Hill searches “dentist near me” is the harder part. That depends on how well your profile matches the search, how close you are, and how well known your business is.

## What helps you show up sooner
- Pick the right main category. It tells Google what you are.
- Fill in everything: hours, service area, phone, website and real photos.
- Keep your name, address and phone the same on your website and in directories.
- Ask happy customers for reviews, and reply to them.

## Common questions
Q: Do I need a storefront to be on Google Maps?
A: No. Service businesses that travel to customers can set a service area and hide their address, as Google's guidelines allow.
Q: Why can't I find my business on the map?
A: It may not be verified yet, it may be hidden by a policy issue, or it may just not rank for that search yet. Search your exact business name first.
Q: Does my website affect my Maps listing?
A: Yes. Google says prominence is partly based on information from across the web, and a clear website linked from your profile helps Google understand what you do.
