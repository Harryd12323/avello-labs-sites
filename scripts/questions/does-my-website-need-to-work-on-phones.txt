## The short answer
Yes. Plenty of local searches happen on phones, often by someone who wants to call right now. Google says it uses the mobile version of a site's content for indexing and ranking. So the phone version is the one that counts.

A slow or awkward page loses people before they call.

## What working on phones means
- Text you can read without zooming
- A phone number you can tap to call
- Buttons big enough for a thumb
- Pages that load fast, even on a weak signal
- A contact form that's quick to fill in

## How to check yours
Open your site on your own phone, then try to call yourself from it. Then run our [free site check](/check/). Google also measures how real visitors experience loading and layout with a set of metrics called Core Web Vitals. Picture someone in a Cherry Hill parking lot, or out on a South Jersey job site, trying to reach you. Could they?
