## The short answer
Both can work. Website builders have made it possible to put up a decent site on your own. The real question is whether you have the time to build it well and keep it up afterward.

If you'd rather spend those hours running your business, or you need the site to bring in calls, hiring someone usually makes more sense.

## Doing it yourself
It's a good fit if your business is simple, you like this kind of thing and you'll actually keep it current. Our guide on [Wix, Squarespace and custom sites](/blog/wix-vs-squarespace-vs-custom-website/) compares the options.

## Hiring someone
Ask what happens after launch. Who fixes things, who makes changes, and who checks the site still works? A site that's built and then left alone tends to go stale. That's why we build sites and then look after them every month on a [care plan](/plans/).

## Questions to ask before you hire
- Will the domain and accounts be in my name?
- Can I see sites you've built?
- What happens after launch, and what does it cost each month?

If you're in Cherry Hill or anywhere in South Jersey and want to start on your own, that's fine. You can switch later.

## Common questions
Q: Can I build it myself now and switch later?
A: Yes. Keep your domain in your own name from the start and it's much easier to move later.
Q: How do I pick a web designer?
A: Look at their past work, ask who you'll actually talk to and ask what happens after launch. Our guide on [choosing a web designer](/blog/choose-web-designer-small-business/) goes through it.
Q: How long does it take if I hire you?
A: A new site can be live in about a week once we have your content.
