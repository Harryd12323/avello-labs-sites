## The short answer
Local SEO is how you show up when someone nearby searches for what you do. Think “roofer near me” or “dentist in Cherry Hill”. It's the same idea as regular SEO, with a focus on location.

There are two places to show up. The map results, driven mostly by your Google Business Profile. And the regular results below, driven by your website.

## What it covers
- **Your Google Business Profile:** the right category, accurate hours, real photos and reviews.
- **Your website:** a clear page for each service, and for the areas you serve, that works well on a phone.
- **Matching details everywhere.** Your name, address and phone should be the same on your site, your profile and directories.

Google says local results are based mainly on relevance, distance and prominence. Distance you can't change. The other two are where the work goes.

## Common questions
Q: Is local SEO different from regular SEO?
A: It uses the same foundations. Local SEO adds the parts that matter for nearby searches, like your Google Business Profile, reviews and location pages.
Q: Can I do local SEO myself?
A: Yes, the basics are something you can do yourself. The hard part is keeping it up every month while running your business.
Q: Does local SEO cost money?
A: Your Google Business Profile is free. The cost is the time to do the work well, yours or someone else's.
