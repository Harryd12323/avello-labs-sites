## The short answer
SEO, search engine optimization, helps your pages show up in a list of search results. AEO, answer engine optimization, helps AI tools understand your business well enough to name it in an answer.

They overlap a lot. AI answers often draw on the same pages that do well in normal search. So we treat them as one ongoing job.

## Where they differ
- **SEO** cares about where you rank: your titles, your pages, your Google Business Profile and reviews.
- **AEO** adds a focus on how your information reads when it's pulled out and summarized somewhere else. Plain answers to real questions, structured data and details that match everywhere.

## Common questions
Q: Is AEO replacing SEO?
A: No. AEO builds on SEO. Without the SEO basics, AI tools have less to go on.
Q: Is AEO the same as GEO?
A: Pretty much. GEO, generative engine optimization, is another name for the same idea: helping AI tools find and use your information.
Q: Can anyone promise AI will mention my business?
A: No. AI tools decide their own answers, and those answers change. What you can do is make your business easy to find and easy to trust.
