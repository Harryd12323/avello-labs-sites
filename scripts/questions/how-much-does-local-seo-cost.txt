## The short answer
There's no set price for local SEO. It depends on how many businesses you're up against and how much work your website and Google Business Profile need before they can compete.

That's why we quote local SEO after we've looked at where you stand. If you want it done every month, our Growth plan puts site care and steady SEO work together. The details are on [our plans page](/plans/).

## What you're paying for
- Getting the basics right: your business details, page titles and Google Business Profile
- Pages that match what people search for, like one page for each service you offer
- Keeping it up as Google changes and other businesses improve their own sites

## Why it's monthly work
Simple fixes, like correcting your business details, can help within weeks. Real ranking gains usually take a few months of steady work. A roofer in Cherry Hill trying to show up across South Jersey has more ground to cover than a shop with two rivals down the road.

## How to tell if a price is fair
Ask what gets done each month, in plain words. Ask for a report that shows it. And be careful with anyone who promises you the top spot. Nobody controls Google's rankings, so that promise can't be kept honestly.
