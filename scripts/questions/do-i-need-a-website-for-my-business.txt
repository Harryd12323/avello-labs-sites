## The short answer
If you want people to find you when they search, yes. Plenty of people look a business up online before they call, visit or buy. A website is the one place you control where you explain what you do, show your work and make it easy to get in touch.

A Google Business Profile and a social page help too. They work best pointing people to a site of your own.

## What a website does that a listing can't
- Gives each service its own page, so you match more of what people search for
- Answers the questions customers ask before they call
- Gives Google and AI tools clear facts about your business, in your words
