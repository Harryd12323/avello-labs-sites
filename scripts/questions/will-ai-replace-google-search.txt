## The short answer
Nobody can tell you for sure, and we'd be wary of anyone who claims to. Right now people use both. Some ask ChatGPT for a recommendation. Many still search Google, and Google now shows AI-written summaries, called AI Overviews, above a lot of its results.

So it isn't really one or the other. The question is whether your business shows up in both.

## What stays the same
AI tools build their answers from what they can find on the web: your website, your Google Business Profile, reviews and other sites that mention you. Those are the same things that help you on Google. Clear facts, matching details everywhere and a good reputation help wherever people look.

## Common questions
Q: Should I stop working on Google and focus on AI?
A: No. Google is still where many people search, and the work overlaps a lot. Good SEO is most of good AEO.
Q: How do I see what AI says about my business?
A: Ask a few AI tools the questions your customers would ask, like “best plumber in Cherry Hill”. Try different wording, and check again every so often.
Q: Can I pay to be recommended by AI?
A: Some AI tools are starting to show ads, labeled as ads. The regular answers are decided by the tools themselves.
