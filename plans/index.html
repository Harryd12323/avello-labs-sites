Why a monthly plan

A website isn't a sign you hang once. It runs on software that needs updates, and it describes a business that keeps changing. Left alone, things go wrong quietly. A form stops sending. A certificate runs out and browsers warn people away. Last year's hours are still up.

You usually find out when a customer mentions it. Or when they don't call at all. A monthly plan means someone is watching, so you can get on with your work.

Common questions about the plans

Do I need a plan if my site is brand new?

Yes. New sites still need updates, monitoring and backups. The first few months are also when you'll most want to change things based on what customers ask.

What's the difference between care and Growth?

Care keeps the website itself healthy: hosting, monitoring, backups, and on Pro and Premium your edits and a report. Growth includes everything in Pro care, plus monthly work on how people find you on Google and in AI answers.

Is there a contract?

No. Plans are month to month, with no contract. You can cancel anytime.

Can you look after a site you didn't build?

Often, yes. Book the free review and we'll look at what you have. If it makes more sense to fix it than start over, we'll say so.

Will the Growth plan get me to the top of Google?

Nobody can promise that, and you should be wary of anyone who does. Google decides rankings, and some of it, like how close a searcher is to you, nobody controls. We do steady work on the things known to help, and show you what we did each month.

How do I know it's worth it?
