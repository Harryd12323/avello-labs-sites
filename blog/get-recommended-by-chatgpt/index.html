How to get your business recommended by ChatGPT

Wondering how to get my business recommended by ChatGPT? It starts with letting ChatGPT's search reach your site, then making your pages easy to read and easy to trust.

By Avello Labs · October 25, 2026 · 6 min read

In short

  • ChatGPT uses its own web crawler to find sites for search answers. If your site blocks it, you can't show up in those answers.
  • Clear pages, consistent business details and a solid Google Business Profile give it something to trust.
  • Nobody outside OpenAI can promise a recommendation, so focus on the basics you control.

How ChatGPT finds businesses when it searches the web

ChatGPT doesn't only answer from memory. When someone asks for something current or local, like "who fixes furnaces near Cherry Hill," it can search the web and pull from pages it finds.

OpenAI runs a few different crawlers. A crawler is a program that visits web pages and reads them, the same way Google's does. OpenAI's documentation names two that matter to you:

  • OAI-SearchBot. OpenAI says this one is used to surface websites in ChatGPT's search features.
  • GPTBot. This one is tied to training OpenAI's AI models, and OpenAI says each setting is independent of the others.

That second point is useful. You can allow the search crawler and still turn off the training one. OpenAI also says sites that opt out of OAI-SearchBot won't be shown in ChatGPT search answers, though they can still appear as navigational links.

What we can't tell you is the exact formula ChatGPT uses to pick one business over another. OpenAI hasn't published a ranking recipe that we could find. So be wary of anyone who says they know it, or who promises you'll be named. What we can do is remove the obvious blockers and make your business easy to understand.

Fix this first: make sure ChatGPT is allowed in

Many sites block AI crawlers by accident. A security plugin, a hosting setting or an old file called robots.txt can do it. Robots.txt is a small text file on your site that tells crawlers which pages they may visit.

OpenAI recommends allowing OAI-SearchBot in your robots.txt if you want your site to appear in search results. It also recommends allowing requests from its published IP ranges. An IP range is the set of internet addresses a crawler visits from. If your host or firewall blocks them, the crawler never gets in, even when robots.txt says yes.

Here is what to do:

  1. Run our AI crawler check on your site, or start with our free site check.
  2. If OAI-SearchBot is blocked, ask whoever manages your site to allow it.
  3. Ask your host whether a firewall or bot filter is turning crawlers away.
  4. Decide separately whether you want GPTBot allowed. That choice is yours and doesn't affect the search crawler.

Make your pages easy to read and easy to trust

Once the door is open, the crawler has to find something worth repeating. Think of it as a very fast reader who has never heard of you.

Say plainly what you do and where

Put it in words on the page, not only in an image or a logo. A roofing company in Gloucester County should say "roof repair and replacement in Gloucester County" on its home page and have a page for each service it offers. A dental office should name its treatments and the towns it serves.

Give each service its own page

One page called "Services" with a long list is hard to quote. A page for "emergency AC repair" or "teeth whitening" can answer one question well. Write the questions customers actually ask, like how long a job takes or what to do before an appointment, and answer them directly.

Keep your business details the same everywhere

Your name, address, phone number and hours should match on your site, your Google listing and the directories you appear in. If they disagree, any system trying to confirm who you are has less reason to trust you. Our post on why your Google Business Profile matters covers the listing side.

Add structured data

Structured data, also called schema, is code that labels your details for machines: this is the business name, this is the phone number, these are the hours. It doesn't show up for visitors. Our local business schema generator builds it for you to copy in. We can't say how much weight ChatGPT gives it, so treat it as good housekeeping, not a trick.

Keep it current

An old hours listing or a service page that mentions a discontinued offer is a trust problem. Check your pages every few months. This is part of what ongoing website care handles.

Build trust beyond your own site

AI tools that search the web read more than your pages. They can also see what others say about you. That's why the following matter for a local business:

  • Real reviews on your Google profile, with replies from you.
  • Listings in local directories and trade groups that are accurate.
  • Mentions on local sites, such as a chamber of commerce page or a local news story.
  • Photos and descriptions that match what you actually do.

None of this is new. It's what good local SEO already asks for, and it overlaps heavily with AI search. If you want the bigger picture, read why AI answers matter and see how SEO fits in. For how we approach this work, see our AEO service.

What to skip

  • Paying for "ChatGPT ranking" packages. No one can buy a spot in answers.
  • Stuffing pages with phrases. Pages written for robots read badly to people too.
  • Blocking everything out of caution. Blocking the search crawler removes you from ChatGPT's search answers.
  • Checking once and forgetting. Hosts, plugins and settings change.

Common questions

Can I submit my business directly to ChatGPT?

Not that we know of. OpenAI hasn't published a submission form for local businesses. The practical route is allowing its search crawler and keeping your site and listings accurate.

Should I block GPTBot?

That's your call. OpenAI says GPTBot and OAI-SearchBot are separate settings, so you can allow search visibility while disallowing training use.

How do I know if ChatGPT is already mentioning my business?

Ask it the questions your customers would ask, such as "best plumber in Burlington County," and see what comes back. Answers can vary, so try a few wordings and don't read too much into one result.

How long until ChatGPT recommends me?

No one can say. It depends on the crawler reaching your site and on things outside your control. Fix the blockers and the basics, then keep them current.

Do I need a new website for this?

Often not. Many businesses just need the crawler unblocked and clearer service pages. If your site is slow, hard to edit or hard to read, a rebuild may help.
