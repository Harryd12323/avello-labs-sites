Google has announced a December core update. Here is what to do

Google has announced its December 2026 core update before it starts. Here is what the Google December core update means for your local business website.

News · By Harris, Avello Labs · November 25, 2026 · 3 min read

In short

  • Google says a core update (a broad change to how its search systems rank pages) is coming in December.
  • It is aimed at understanding longer, more complex questions and at personalized results.
  • Google gave no exact start date, so do not change your site in a panic.
  • Note your current results now so you can compare later.

What happened

Search Engine Journal reports that Google posted an early notice on its Search Status Dashboard. That is the page where Google lists changes to Search. The notice says a core update will be released in December.

Google says the update improves how its systems understand and rank relevant content. That includes longer and more complicated questions. It also adjusts results for people who have chosen to get personalized search. The update applies everywhere Google Search is available.

There is no start date and no length for the rollout yet. The notice came shortly after Google said its November core update had finished. So two updates are landing close together.

What it means for a local business

A core update does not target one site. It changes how Google judges pages across the board. Some sites move up, some move down, and many barely change.

The timing matters here. If two updates land close together, it is hard to tell which one caused a change. Google's own advice is to wait at least a week after an update ends, then compare that week to the week before it started.

Say you run a plumbing company in Camden County. Your website calls dip in early December. Was it the update, the holidays, or the weather? Without notes, you will not know. Personalization also means two people can see slightly different results for the same search, so one quick check on your own phone does not tell the whole story.

What to do about it

  1. Write down where you stand today. Note the main searches your customers use, and look at your Google Search Console numbers (Google's free report on how your site appears in search).
  2. Add a note in Search Console for the date November's update finished, and another when December's start date is known.
  3. Do not rewrite your whole site because of an announcement. Wait until the update finishes, then compare.
  4. Make sure each service page clearly answers the real questions customers ask, in plain words. Our local SEO checklist is a good place to start.
  5. Check that your Google Business Profile is accurate and your site loads fast and works on phones. Our free site check can look at the site side for you.
  6. If results change a lot after the update, get someone to look at the cause before you act. We cover this in our SEO service.
