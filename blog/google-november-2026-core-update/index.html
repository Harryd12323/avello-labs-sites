Google's November core update is rolling out. Here's what to do.

Google's November core update is rolling out now, and AI Mode just switched models too. Here is what the Google November core update means for a local business site.

News · By Harris, Avello Labs · November 16, 2026 · 3 min read

In short

  • Google began a core update (a broad change to how it ranks pages) on Nov. 10, and it can take up to two weeks to finish.
  • Google also changed the default model behind AI Mode on Nov. 12.
  • Don't change your site in a panic. Write down the dates and compare your numbers after the update ends.
  • ChatGPT now lets businesses claim a profile and see mentions and clicks, in beta.

What happened

Search Engine Journal reports on several changes in search this week. The big one is that Google started rolling out its November 2026 core update on Nov. 10. It is the third core update of the year. Google's status page says it applies worldwide and may take up to two weeks to finish. As of Nov. 12 it was still going.

Two days later, on Nov. 12, Google made a new model, Gemini 4 Hydrogen, the default in AI Mode, its chat-style search. Google also uses it for some harder questions in AI Overviews, the AI summaries at the top of results. Google says it connects people to a broader range of links. It did not say how many.

Separately, OpenAI opened a beta of Business Hub. Eligible businesses can claim a ChatGPT profile, confirm their identity, and get reports on how frequently ChatGPT brings them up and how many visitors it sends their way.

What it means for a local business

Rankings can move around while an update is rolling out. That does not mean your site is broken, and it does not mean you should rewrite it today.

Say you run an HVAC company in Burlington County. Your page for furnace repair drops a few spots on Tuesday and recovers on Friday. If you had rebuilt the page on Tuesday, you would never know what caused what.

Google's advice is to wait at least a full week after the rollout ends. Then compare that week in Search Console (Google's free tool showing how people find your site) with the week before the update began.

The AI Mode change adds a second date to keep in mind. If your numbers shift in November, it could be either event, or neither. Business Hub is also worth knowing about, but the numbers are OpenAI's own counts. A click it records is not a confirmed visit, and claiming a profile does not promise you more mentions.

What to do about it

  1. Write down two dates: Nov. 10 for the core update and Nov. 12 for the AI Mode change.
  2. Hold off on big edits to pages that are working. Fix real problems, like a broken contact form or wrong hours, as normal.
  3. Once the update is finished, wait a full week. Then compare that week to the week before Nov. 10 in Search Console.
  4. Keep your Google Business Profile accurate. Our guide on why it matters covers the basics.
  5. Check that AI tools can reach your site. Our AI crawler check shows this in a minute.
  6. If you want to be recommended by ChatGPT, see how that works. Our SEO service page explains how we help.
  7. Not sure where your site stands? Run our free site check for a quick look.
