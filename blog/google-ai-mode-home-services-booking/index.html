Google AI Mode now links home service searchers to booking and quote pages

Google AI Mode now offers direct booking and quote links for home service businesses, through a short list of partner platforms. Here is what that means for your business and what to check this week.

News · By Harris, Avello Labs · November 23, 2026 · 3 min read

In short

  • Google AI Mode can now show booking and quote links for home service businesses, but only through partner platforms.
  • Partners named so far are Angi, Thumbtack, Jobber and Housecall Pro, with ServiceTitan listed as coming soon.
  • Each platform has its own rules, so check your account instead of assuming you are in.
  • Your website and Google Business Profile still matter, because they are what people and AI tools see first.

What happened

Search Engine Journal reports that Google has added direct links in AI Mode (its conversational search, where people ask a question and get an answer instead of a list of links) for home services. A searcher can ask for a handyman, and AI Mode shows local providers with ratings, short descriptions and, for some, open appointment times. Google's Search product VP, Robby Stein, said it is live in the U.S.

The links run through partners. Angi and Thumbtack are marketplaces. Jobber and Housecall Pro are field service software, the programs many contractors use to schedule jobs and send invoices. ServiceTitan is listed as coming soon.

Details differ. Jobber says eligible U.S. customers can take these requests now, and Housecall Pro says its integration is coming soon. Angi sends people to the pro's Angi profile to request a quote. Thumbtack had not announced its plans as of the report.

What it means for a local business

Whether you can take bookings from Google's AI search now depends partly on the software you already use. Say you run a plumbing company in Camden County and schedule jobs in Jobber. Jobber's help page says you need a paid plan, a full address, and a Google Business Profile connected to it. If your Jobber address does not match your profile, you may not show up.

If you use none of the listed platforms, AI Mode does not offer you a booking path at the moment, according to the story. That is a snapshot of today.

The story also notes that Google has not said how it picks which businesses to show. Being on a partner platform is not a ticket in. Nobody can promise a result.

These links are one more door customers might use. Your website, your Google Business Profile and word of mouth are still open, and you control them.

What to do about it

  1. Find out which platform you use for jobs or leads, and read its AI Mode eligibility rules.
  2. If you use Jobber, confirm your address matches your Google Business Profile. Our post on why your Google Business Profile matters explains the basics.
  3. If you use Housecall Pro or Angi, check your account or profile for AI Mode status and keep the information current.
  4. Make sure your website clearly lists your services, service area and phone number. Our AEO service is about being recommended by AI tools.
