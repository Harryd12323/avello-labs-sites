Google's new default AI Mode model: what local businesses should know

Google changed the default model behind AI Mode, and that matters if you track how AI tools mention your business. Here is what happened and what to do.

News · By Harris, Avello Labs · November 21, 2026 · 3 min read

In short

  • Google made a new model, Gemini 4 Hydrogen, the default in AI Mode for all users worldwide on November 12.
  • Google says it also handles the hardest questions in AI Overviews, the AI answers at the top of search results.
  • Google says answers will link out to a broader range of websites, but it gave no numbers.
  • You don't need to rebuild anything. Note the date and keep an eye on your own reports.

What happened

Search Engine Journal reports that Google has switched AI Mode over to a new model, Gemini 4 Hydrogen, for everyone. AI Mode is Google's chat-style search, where people ask longer questions and get a written answer with links.

Google says the new model copes better with questions that have several parts, contain typos or are very specific. It also says Hydrogen improves query fan-out. That is when Google quietly runs many smaller searches at once, one for each part of a question, then pulls the results together. Google says this connects people to a wider range of helpful links.

Google's head of Search product also said Hydrogen is being used for the hardest questions in AI Overviews. Google hasn't said which questions count as "hardest."

The change landed two days into Google's November core update, a broad update to how Google ranks pages.

What it means for a local business

For most owners, nothing breaks today. Your site is not penalized, and nothing here says you must change anything.

But a ranking update and a new AI model started within days of each other. If your visits or calls shift this month, it is hard to know which one caused it. Say you run an HVAC company in Burlington County and notice a change in late November. Without dates written down, you can't tell which update is behind it.

Google says more links will appear in AI answers. It gave no figures, so treat that as a claim, not a result.

What to do about it

  1. Write down two dates: November 10, when the core update began, and November 12, when the AI Mode model changed.
  2. Compare your website visits, calls and form submissions before and after each date.
  3. Search a few questions your customers ask, such as "emergency AC repair near me," in AI Mode. Note whether your business is mentioned or linked.
  4. Keep your services, service areas, hours and contact details clear on real pages. Our AEO service helps AI tools recommend you.
