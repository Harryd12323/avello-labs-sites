Should I pay monthly for website care or just fix things when they break?

TL;DR

If your site matters for bringing in customers, monthly care usually makes more sense. Problems get caught early, when they're cheap to fix, instead of when a customer tells you.

The short answer

Websites tend to break quietly. A form stops sending, a page goes missing, a security certificate runs out. If you only fix things when they break, you usually find out when a customer mentions it. Or when they don't call at all.

Monthly care means someone is watching. Most problems are cheap to fix early and expensive to discover late.

When pay as you go is fine

If your site is simple, rarely changes and doesn't bring in much business, fixing things as they come up can be enough. Just check it yourself every so often, including sending yourself a test message through the contact form.

What a month of care looks like

  • We check the site is up, fast and secure
  • We apply updates and confirm nothing broke
  • We send a test message through every form
  • We update anything that changed, like hours, services or photos

Say you run a contracting business in Cherry Hill and switch to winter hours. On a Pro or Premium plan, you tell us and we update it. Nobody in South Jersey shows up to a locked door because your site still says you're open.
