How often should I post on my Google Business Profile?

TL;DR

Google doesn't set a number. A steady rhythm you can keep, like a post a month plus fresh photos, beats a burst of posts followed by silence.

The short answer

Google doesn't say how often you should post. What it does say is that a complete, accurate profile helps people find you. Posts are one way to show your profile is looked after.

A good habit for a busy owner is one useful post a month, plus new photos when you have them. Keep it going. A profile that went quiet two years ago tells people less than one updated last week.

What to post

  • A seasonal service. An HVAC company in Cherry Hill might post about furnace checks in the fall.
  • A recent job, with a photo you're allowed to share.
  • A holiday schedule or a change in hours.
  • An answer to a question customers keep asking.

More than posts

Posts are the visible part. Just as important: your hours are right, your services are listed, new photos go up and reviews get a reply. Common questions

Do posts help me rank higher?

Google doesn't say posts are a ranking factor. They help people who find your profile see that you're active, which can help them choose you.

Can I post the same thing every month?

You can, but it won't tell anyone much. Short and specific posts work better than repeats.

Should I post offers?

If you have a real offer, yes. Google Business Profile has a post type for offers.
