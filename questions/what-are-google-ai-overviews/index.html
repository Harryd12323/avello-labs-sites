What are Google AI Overviews, and do they affect my business?

TL;DR

AI Overviews are short AI-written answers Google shows at the top of some searches, with links to the sources. Google says the same SEO basics apply. There's no special trick to get in.

The short answer

AI Overviews are the summaries Google writes with AI and shows above the regular results for some searches. They answer the question directly and link to the pages they drew from.

For a local business, they matter because people may read the summary before they ever see the list of businesses. If your site is one of the sources, your name can come up there.

How to give yourself a better chance

Google's guidance for site owners says there are no extra requirements to appear in AI Overviews. The usual SEO basics apply.

  • Pages Google can crawl and index, with the main text in the page.
  • Clear answers to the questions customers ask, in their own words.
  • Accurate business details that match your Google Business Profile.

Common questions

Can I turn off AI Overviews for my site?

Google offers settings such as nosnippet that limit how your content is shown, but they can also limit your regular listing. For most local businesses it's better to be included.

Do AI Overviews show for every search?

No. Google shows them for some searches and not others, and that changes over time.

Will AI Overviews mean fewer people visit my website?

Some quick questions may get answered on Google itself. People looking to hire someone nearby still need to pick a business and contact it.
