What gets in the way

  • Beautiful pieces, thin product pages. A photo and a title don't give Google or AI shopping tools enough to show your work.
  • Services get lost. Repairs, resizing, appraisals and custom design often hide on one line, so a person searching for a ring resize in Cherry Hill never finds you.
  • The online store and the shop tell different stories. Different hours, missing pieces or old prices make people unsure.
  • Photos load slowly on phones. Big images are lovely on a desktop and painful on a phone.

What we do about it

We write product pages that describe each piece the way a buyer would ask about it: the metal, the stones, the size, how it's made. That's what search engines and AI shopping tools read.

We give each service its own page, so repairs, custom work and appraisals can each be found on their own.

We keep your store, your Google Business Profile and your shop details matching, and we keep photos sharp but quick to load.

Every month we look after the site and the listings, so new pieces and holiday hours show up when they should.
