Free instant website check

Enter your website's address. In a few seconds you'll get a score out of 100 and simple fixes for the SEO and AEO basics that help people find you on Google and in AI answers.

Free, no sign-up. We check the page you enter, plus its robots.txt and sitemap. We don't store the address.

What the check looks at

  • Secure connection: the page loads over HTTPS, and the http:// version redirects to it.
  • Allowed in Google: nothing on the page tells search engines to stay away.
  • Page title and meta description: present, and a good length for search results.
  • Mobile setup: the page is set up to fit phone screens.
  • Main heading: one clear H1 that says what the page is about.
  • Business details for search engines: schema markup that describes your business, which also helps AI tools get your details right.
  • robots.txt and sitemap: search engines can find and crawl your pages.
  • Image descriptions: images have alt text for screen readers and image search.
  • Page size: the page's HTML isn't unusually heavy.

What a quick check can't tell you. This looks at one page's code as your server sends it. It can't judge your content, reviews, competition or rankings, and it doesn't measure how fast images and scripts load. Sites that build their pages with JavaScript can score lower here than they deserve. For the full picture, ask for the free full report after your check.
